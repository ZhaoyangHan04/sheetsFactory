# LP/GP partnership waterfall

This optional module allocates actual funded parent cash after its debt and fee priority. It does not create capital, estimate programme collections or liquidate continuing investments. The default holding model remains unchanged when `waterfall_spec=None`.

`initial_state(policy)` starts the dated LP/GP capital accounts. `allocate_year(policy, state, year, capital_called, distributable_cash)` returns `state`, `annual`, `journal` and invariant `checks`, without mutating its inputs. Call it once per fiscal year, in order. Calls are the total already-funded partner contribution, split according to the explicit LP fraction. Available cash must be the actual post-financing amount above the parent minimum.

The annual financial outputs include partner contributions, preferred return, capital returns, GP catch-up, residual shares, total partner distributions, closing capital/preference claims and dated LP XIRR. `capital_distribution`, `profit_distribution` and `total_distribution` are the parent accounting bridge. The returned debit-positive journal contains distributions only: contributions are already posted by the parent. Capital returns debit paid-in capital; all other distributions debit retained earnings. Neither changes net income.

`contract.PUBLIC_RULES` contains the complete custom business policy. Monetary postings use Decimal half-up cents. Preferred return is noncompounding and excludes current-year calls. The GP cumulative catch-up target includes preference and all historical profit distributions, excluding capital returns. A permanent carry flip starts the year after both capitals are returned and the LP hurdle is met. New calls after a flip do not undo it. Actual/365 XIRR is calculated independently by bracketing and bisection, with a unique-root proof for the permitted single-sign-change cash-flow sequence. Qualification uses unrounded XNPV at the hurdle.

`excel.emit_waterfall` independently emits formulas from provided policy and live parent references. It does not call the Decimal allocator. Its bindings mark execution flags, carry-state displays and hurdle XNPV as provided, rather than financial reconstruction targets. The optional holding integration emits the partner sheets, deducts their distributions from parent cash, paid-in capital, retained earnings and financing cash flow, and passes the resulting closing balances to the next fiscal year. Consolidated cash and equity include the same external partner distributions once.

`config/derived_partner_waterfall_v1.json` is the original 4% preference / 2% hurdle contract. Its current-parent preflight cannot reach capital return and must not be claimed to activate all tiers. Version-2 scenario configuration explicitly uses a 2% preference / 1% hurdle; it does not replace the original evidence. Source selection must require actual observed business regimes after all connected programme costs and collections, then real independent workbook validation. Hand fixtures and partial reachability checks are not task or difficulty qualifications.
