# Advanced project tax contract, prototype v1

This is an explicit hypothetical management model, not a statement of tax law or accounting standards. It adds genuine timing differences to the existing 36-month project and covenant model. A source or task is not qualified by implementing this document. Existing disabled-mode outputs must remain unchanged.

## Configuration and public assumptions

The optional `advanced_tax_parameters` object has exactly these keys: `advanced_tax_enabled` (0 or 1), `initial_tax_rate_y1`, `initial_tax_rate_y2`, `initial_tax_rate_y3`, `refresh_tax_rate_y1`, `refresh_tax_rate_y2`, `refresh_tax_rate_y3`, `interest_deduction_ebitda_fraction` (0..1), `nol_carryforward_years` (integer 1 or 2). Each asset's three nonnegative fractions sum exactly to one. Prototype defaults are initial .50/.30/.20, refresh .60/.30/.10, interest fraction .30, NOL carry years 1. These appear as explicit Scenario inputs beginning at row 63. Advanced mode requires opening NOL and opening retained earnings zero; initial deferred tax, interest carry and tax depreciation balances are zero.

All amounts are posted cents using half-up rounding at each named monetary line. The original monthly book depreciation, funding, debt, covenant, reserve and valuation rules still apply. Only the old annual current-tax/NOL rule and no-deferred-tax assertion are replaced. Annual tax calculations occur only at December month end. The monthly NOL, interest carry, deferred asset and deferred liability balances carry their last posted balances between December events. No January write-off is implied. Current cash tax is a nonnegative payment. Total book tax and deferred tax expense are signed: negative denotes a tax benefit. Cash Flow principal helpers and all other old magnitude conventions remain unchanged.

## Annual tax event in this order

1. Sum the year's posted monthly EBITDA, book depreciation and cash interest on all facilities, separately. Annual book EBT is EBITDA minus book depreciation minus cash interest.
2. Tax depreciation follows calendar-year asset vintages, beginning in the calendar year containing each asset's service month, with a full stated annual rate and no monthly proration in the first service year. A vintage not yet in service has zero tax depreciation. In the first and second service calendar years use cost times that year's fraction, rounded to cents and capped at remaining basis. In the third service calendar year deduct all remaining basis, so cent residuals are exhausted. After that charge zero. Initial cost exists from month zero, refresh tax cost enters only in its paid month; pre-service cost remains in tax basis. Closing tax basis equals total acquired asset cost less cumulative posted tax depreciation. Do not deduct construction or capex separately from taxable income.
3. Interest capacity is positive annual EBITDA times the configured fraction, rounded to cents. Use capacity against carried interest vintages oldest first, then against current-year cash interest. All unused current interest becomes that year's carry vintage. Interest carry does not expire within this horizon. Do not deduct carried interest twice. Allowed interest equals old carry consumed plus current-year interest consumed.
4. Taxable income before NOL is annual EBITDA minus tax depreciation minus allowed interest. At this December event discard old NOL vintages whose current model year minus creation year exceeds `nol_carryforward_years`; carry years 1 means a year-1 loss can be used at year-2 year end but is discarded before any year-3 use. Use eligible remaining old vintages oldest first, up to the smaller of their total and the cent-rounded positive taxable-income-before-NOL times the existing `nol_utilization_cap`. Negative taxable income creates a new current-year NOL; it cannot be used during its creation year. Closing NOL is old opening NOL less expiry and use plus new loss. Cash current tax equals positive taxable income after NOL times tax rate, rounded to cents. All NOL and interest vintage helper amounts are nonnegative magnitudes; a future vintage is zero.
5. Closing gross DTA is the SUM of three separately cent-rounded components: closing NOL times tax rate; closing interest carry times tax rate; positive (closing tax basis minus closing book net PPE) times tax rate. Closing DTL is positive (closing book net PPE minus closing tax basis) times tax rate, rounded to cents. The hypothetical model recognizes these gross amounts in full with no valuation allowance or netting; expiry therefore writes off the corresponding NOL benefit. Deferred tax expense equals change in DTL minus change in DTA. Total book income-tax expense equals cash current tax plus deferred tax expense. Do not deduct deferred tax to obtain taxable income.
6. Book net income subtracts total book tax from book EBT. The journal posts the DTA/DTL changes as balanced noncash entries. Cash CFO deducts current cash tax only. Indirect CFO adds signed deferred tax expense back to net income along with book depreciation and the existing working-capital bridge. Covenant CFADS continues to use cash CFO plus cash interest minus refresh capex. DTA is a separate asset and DTL a separate liability; do not use an equity or balance-sheet plug. Changes in DTA/DTL are not entity cash flows.
7. Exit EV, seller fee and owner return rules remain unchanged. The sale multiple already incorporates the purchaser's view of tax attributes. Do not add DTA or subtract DTL again in the owner sale bridge. Cash and debt enter that bridge under the existing contract.

## Stable interface between independent implementations

Numerical ledger calculations must not call or import Excel forecasting calculations. Shared field names and row metadata are allowed. The emitter must not call the ledger or embed ledger answers as formulas or constants.

Monthly ledger keys added: `current_tax`, `deferred_tax_expense`, `dta_close`, `dtl_close`, `nol_expired`, `interest_cf_close`. Existing `tax` becomes signed TOTAL BOOK income-tax expense only when advanced mode is enabled. Existing NOL keys remain opening/use/add/close under the rules above. `annual` includes monthly sum of current_tax, deferred_tax_expense and nol_expired, and December stocks dta_close, dtl_close, interest_cf_close. `tax_annual` is a list of three dicts with `year` plus the names below. All names denote money except year.

Annual aggregate keys, in this order: `ebitda`, `book_depreciation`, `cash_interest`, `book_ebt`, `initial_tax_depreciation`, `refresh_tax_depreciation`, `tax_depreciation`, `tax_basis_close`, `interest_capacity`, `interest_cf_open`, `interest_cf_used`, `current_interest_deducted`, `current_interest_carried`, `interest_deduction`, `interest_cf_close`, `taxable_before_nol`, `nol_open`, `nol_expired`, `nol_use_cap`, `nol_used`, `nol_new`, `nol_close`, `current_tax`, `book_net_ppe`, `dta_nol`, `dta_interest`, `dta_basis`, `dta_close`, `dtl_close`, `deferred_tax_expense`, `book_tax`.

For vintage v = 1,2,3, annual dict additionally includes `nol_v{v}_open`, `nol_v{v}_expired`, `nol_v{v}_used`, `nol_v{v}_close`, `interest_v{v}_open`, `interest_v{v}_used`, `interest_v{v}_close`. Current-year new NOL/current interest carried is included only in that year's respective closing vintage, never its opening amount or old-vintage used amount. Emitter annual `Tax Vintages` sheet uses C/D/E for model years 1/2/3; row 2 is year, subsequent rows follow the aggregate sequence above then, for each v in 1/2/3, the seven vintage fields in the stated order. Monthly Tax row 7 is CURRENT CASH TAX, row 8 NOL expired, row 9 interest carry close, row 10 signed deferred tax expense, row 11 DTA close, row 12 DTL close. Operations row 13 is TOTAL BOOK TAX. Cash Flow row 19 is deferred tax adjustment; Balance Sheet row 21 is DTA, row 22 is DTL. Existing DSRA row 20 stays unchanged. New annual summary rows begin after existing covenant annual rows (row 73); this is source metadata, not a task instruction to change unaffected cells.

Required independent checks: vintage rollforwards, allowed interest <= capacity, no double-use of current/carry interest, NOL expiry order and FIFO, total tax bridge, book/ledger cash and balance-sheet identities, LO-calculated every formula typed/cent equality, second LO stability. Candidate regime screening must report actual creation/use/expiry, carry creation/use, tax basis/book differences, positive and negative deferred expenses, cash-vs-book tax difference and covenant events. Do not assert a regime unless observed. Some source scenarios may legitimately be rejected if a required regime does not occur.
