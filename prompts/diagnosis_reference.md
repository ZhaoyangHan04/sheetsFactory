# Privileged financial reference review

Review every assigned check. The complete evidence remains available at the SHA-bound `private_evidence.full_artifact` path. Its summary is an index, not an approval or a replacement for evidence. Check its SHA before targeted reads. Each compacted value gives its JSON pointer, size and digest; use those pointers to inspect omitted-from-summary details. Do not print the full artifact, workbook, audit cell table, expected-delta list or dependency graph.

First inspect the public business briefs, the financial audit coverage and invariant summaries, the exact recovery/control bindings, the mutation mechanisms and the listed boundary examples. Check custom policy coherence and material edge cases: periods, cash priorities, signs, units, rounding, event boundaries, losses, financing constraints and consolidation. Follow a concrete concern into the complete evidence or its source file. Programmatic checks can inspect every record while returning counts and a few relevant examples.

Successful, hash-verified independent financial audits and exact recovery are evidence of the covered numerical calculations, not a proof that the public task is unique or that every business policy is correct. Review the policies and their connection to the actual workbook. Do not independently recompute every already-audited monetary cell by default. An unverified hash, incomplete audit, failed invariant, unresolved business choice or unsupported root certificate still needs investigation; return unknown or a supported issue when the evidence is insufficient. Never infer clear from a summary's existence.

For debugging, distinguish a single-root execution from a sound separated-root or batched separated-root proof. A batched proof must bind the original and fully mutated graphs, show no within-batch root interference in both, and use actual batch recalculations to establish the stated individual and full-context marginal changes. It does not claim one LibreOffice execution per root or full descendant coverage. Inspect the actual certificate and relevant boundary records when needed. Surviving neighbouring formulas are not automatically leaked answers; an executable shortcut needs separate evidence.

Keep agent behavior, environment failures and task/reference defects separate. Retain every assigned assessment, exact response schema and evidence requirement. A previous reviewer timeout is an operational failure, not evidence that the workbook is defective. Do not use a solver's completion status as the quality decision.
