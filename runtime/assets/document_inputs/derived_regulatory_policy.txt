# Hypothetical incremental regulatory capital reopener, contract v2

This is a fictional incremental resilience investment programme, not a statement of current UK law or an amendment to any licensee's actual allowed revenue. The Ofgem ED2 V5 workbook is an upstream published forecast only. Its original calculations remain visible and unchanged; their size is not counted as new task complexity. Original source: https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/ED2-PCFM-V5.xlsx . Background: ED2 Price Control Financial Handbook v4.0 (June 2026), chapters 2, 3 and 6. The new policies below are supplied hypothetical assumptions.

## Scope and units

The new model covers financial years ending 31 March 2024–2043. It models incremental capital assets, capital-related revenue earned, delayed customer receipts, current tax, borrowing and owner flows. It is a regulatory current-tax ledger, not an IFRS financial statement: tax attributes remain off balance sheet and no deferred tax is recognized. Money is GBP, not GBP millions. Real asset balances are constant 2020/21 pounds. Tax bases, debt, claims, income, cash and equity are nominal pounds. Round every named monetary posting half up to pennies before reuse. Ratios are supplied inputs. Sum separately rounded per-asset nominal amounts. Annual real return is rounded once on the aggregate return base.

## Public inputs and source use

Fifteen investment batches are additional to the source forecast: 2024–2028 load-related, replacement and other-capex budgets, respectively multiplied by public programme shares 8%, 12% and 20%. The benchmark is Totex total allowed capex rows 34–36. Each batch's provided real cost is the rounded product of the published GBP-million amount, one million and programme share. Paid-year nominal cost is that real cost times the paid-year price index, rounded per batch. Source values, shares, real costs and paid-year nominal costs are provided input fields, not unknown outputs.

Load batches commission one year after payment, have a 7-year regulatory life and initially enter the General tax pool. Replacement batches commission two years after payment, have a 9-year regulatory life and enter Special. Other batches commission one year after payment, have a 13-year regulatory life and enter Structures; Structures tax life is 11 years. These schedules are hypothetical. All programme spending qualifies; no opening programme balances, disposals or operating expenses exist.

2024–2028 conversion factors and real WACC are published source observations, rounded to 8 and 12 decimal places respectively. Later conversion factors grow the supplied 2028 factor by the supplied 2% annual assumption and are provided to 8 decimals. Later real WACC is 4%. The claim carrying rate is a provided annual input derived from the real WACC and the annual change in the conversion index. Debt rate is 5%; gearing is 60%. Current tax is 25% through 2031, 28% thereafter. Other annual rates, transfer fractions and collection limits are explicit in the public annual input table; input values control.

## Asset timing and regulatory recovery

At payment, the whole real batch cost enters construction in progress. On the commissioning year it transfers in full into RAV before depreciation. Depreciation is a fixed cent-rounded real cost/life annual charge, half of that rounded charge in the first service year, and full charge in later years capped at remaining RAV. Do not redistribute previous rounding. In 2031, after that year's depreciation, disallow up to GBP10 million of real RAV, oldest commissioned batch first; break same-year ties by the public batch order. This is a regulatory disallowance, not a disposal: no cash proceeds, tax deduction or tax-basis removal arises. Later depreciation retains the original charge and caps at the reduced remaining balance. The disallowance never touches construction in progress.

Annual real return uses the mean of opening RAV and closing RAV after depreciation and disallowance. Convert that rounded real return to nominal at the current index. Convert each batch's depreciation and disallowance to nominal separately before aggregation. No return is earned on construction in progress. Nominal RAV and construction-in-progress values are current-index real balances; their indexation is a noncash revaluation reserve movement, not taxable income. The revaluation movement is the exact bridge from opening nominal asset balances, paid cash, commissioning transfers, nominal depreciation/disallowance to current-index closing balances.

## Nominal tax bases

Before commissioning the batch's original paid-year nominal cost remains tax construction in progress. It is not indexed. On commissioning, transfer that whole original nominal amount to its stated tax pool. General and Special use reducing-balance deductions: the current annual rate applies fully to opening balances and half to new commissioning additions, rounded separately per batch and pool and capped at the available pool balance. In 2030 transfer 30% of each General batch's opening balance, rounded separately, into its Special component before allowances. This is not a realization; it generates neither taxable profit nor cash. The transferred amount receives the full Special annual rate. New General additions in that year do not transfer. Structures uses fixed original nominal cost/11 straight-line charge, first service year half charge, then full capped charges. Tax construction in progress and all closing component balances must remain visible.

## Losses and tax allowance on tax allowance

Claims' opening balances accrue nominal carrying interest at the supplied rate, rounded per claim vintage; this is recognized taxable financing income immediately even during the collection lag. Pre-allowance taxable profit is nominal regulatory depreciation revenue plus nominal allowed return plus claim carrying interest, less cash debt interest and tax capital allowances. Asset indexation, regulatory disallowance, capital cash, debt principal and owner flows do not enter this profit.

The current tax allowance is itself taxable revenue and equals the current notional tax expense. Solve the continuous simultaneous tax/loss amounts before any rounding: a loss deduction cannot exceed available losses or 80% of positive taxable profit including the tax allowance; current tax is the tax rate times positive profit after that deduction. Round the resulting allowance and total loss deduction separately once to pennies. Do not iterate rounded tax values. Negative pre-allowance profit creates a current-year loss and zero tax allowance. New losses cannot offset earlier years or the same year's other vintages beyond this aggregate annual calculation.

At the beginning of each year expire losses more than one year old, then use remaining older vintages oldest first; the separately rounded annual deduction is allocated against penny balances without loss. New losses enter their creation-year closing vintage. Future-vintage cells are zero. No group relief or other deductions exist. Pay current tax in cash in the earning year, whether or not the related revenue has been collected.

## Revenue claims, collection and financing

Each year creates one claim for nominal regulatory depreciation plus nominal allowed return plus that year's tax allowance. Carrying interest adds to its originating older claim, not to the new claim. Opening claims accrue interest before collection. A claim first becomes collectible two years after its creation. Collect eligible claims including their newly accrued interest oldest first, subject to that year's nominal collection cap; the cap is a provided incremental tariff limit. Unused capacity is not carried forward. Remaining principal and interest stay as regulatory receivables. At 2043 no forced settlement, write-off or terminal cash plug occurs; any remaining claim and asset balance is an explicit closing balance.

Draw debt equal to 60% of paid nominal capital cash. Repay the smaller of debt after draws and 60% of nominal regulatory depreciation. Debt interest uses opening debt only. Owner capital supplies exactly a negative residual cash requirement after actual customer receipts, capital spending, debt draws/repayments, interest and current tax; positive residual cash is distributed. This is an explicit unlimited programme funding commitment, not unexplained balancing finance. No cash is retained after this annual owner settlement. Track cumulative owner capital, retained earnings and the separate noncash revaluation reserve.

## Final financial outputs and oracle

Earned revenue includes the new capital claim and carrying interest. Net income deducts nominal regulatory depreciation, disallowance, cash interest and current tax. Claims roll forward by opening plus earned revenue minus receipts. Debt rolls forward by opening plus draws minus repayments. Cash flows and the nominal balance sheet must reconcile exactly; equity equals paid-in capital plus retained earnings plus revaluation reserve. The independent Decimal journal must reproduce every new financial formula cell, all batch tax/RAV and claim/loss vintage outputs, and final income/cash/net assets. Original official forecast outputs are provided reference material, not separately certified by this new ledger. Actual LibreOffice recalculation and two further normalizations must match the ledger and each other. A public-only standalone reconstruction proves reachability; it is not an LLM difficulty score.


Revision 2 changes only the hypothetical programme loss-eligibility window from two following years to one. The prototype two-year window used all losses before expiry; the revised public assumption makes both genuine expiry and FIFO use observable. The two-year customer-collection lag is unchanged.
