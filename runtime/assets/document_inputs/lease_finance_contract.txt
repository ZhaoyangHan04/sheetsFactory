# Hypothetical lease contract v1

The public model uses nominal absolute GBP and240months from1April2023 to31March2043. Fiscal years end in March2024..2043. Every primitive term and event is supplied explicitly; null event fields retain the previous term. This is a hypothetical cash/accounting agreement, not an assertion that an accounting standard supplies unstated policies.

All leases commence at the start of a month. Contract rent is payable at month-end, every stated number of months counted from commencement. Initial direct costs are paid at commencement and added to the right-of-use asset (ROU), without increasing the lease liability. Fixed rent steps are known contractual changes from commencement. Index changes, modifications and partial/full terminations occur at the beginning of their event month, before that month's interest, cash and depreciation. Events occur only at payment-cycle starts; there are no stub periods or multiple events for one lease/month.

The current index level is multiplied by the event's observed index ratio after the lease's per-review floor/cap. Future unknown index events are not anticipated when measuring the liability; the current index and retained exposure are held constant. Already contracted future fixed rent steps are included. An event's supplied new discount rate and/or end month replaces the previous term. Index levels, retained fractions, fixed-step factors and effective monthly discount rates round half up to12decimal places before reuse. Monthly discounting is the effective annual rate converted to one month. Monetary amounts round half up to pennies before reuse. The liability is the cent-rounded sum of unrounded discounted, cent-rounded future contract payments, including the event month's month-end payment. Future periodic cash is the stated payment frequency times that month's cent-rounded monthly rent.

Initial liability equals the future-payment value; initial ROU adds direct costs. An index/modification event adjusts ROU by the change in liability. A partial termination first retains the stated fraction of opening liability and ROU, each rounded separately, and proportionally reduces future rent. The removed liability less removed ROU, less the cash termination fee, is a signed gain. The remaining liability is then remeasured on the revised terms; any downward ROU adjustment beyond zero is an additional gain. A full termination removes all opening ROU/liability, pays the stated fee and stops future rent/interest/depreciation. Fees are immediate book expenses within signed termination gain/loss. No other variable payments, incentives, purchase options or residual guarantees exist.

Monthly interest uses the measured opening liability. Cash settles after interest. At the contractual final payment, cash settles the remaining liability including that month's interest, absorbing only cumulative cent rounding. ROU depreciates straight line over the remaining months from commencement or a remeasurement; its cent-rounded monthly charge remains fixed until the next such event. The final service month depreciates the remaining ROU balance. All leases end within the supplied horizon, without exit values or undisclosed tails.

Total lease cash means recurring rent plus initial direct costs plus termination fees, each counted once. That total is the current tax deduction and the regulatory lease expense eligible for the programme's separately stated allowable fraction and recovery timing. ROU/liability tax bases are zero. Book lease expense is depreciation plus interest less signed termination gain/loss. The taxable-income adjustment to profit after book lease expense is book expense less cash deduction. Neither ROU recognition nor liability remeasurement is an additional cash flow. Opening/closing stocks are first/last monthly balances; other annual amounts sum months. Positive gain increases income; cash, depreciation and interest are positive costs. The complete monthly and annual journal must satisfy ΔROU+depreciation−(Δliability+totalcash−interest)=signed gain. No balancing plug is allowed.
